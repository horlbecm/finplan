Asset Transfer Efficiency Ranking
1
Taxable Brokerage — Stepped-Up Basis
All unrealized gains wiped out at death. Heirs sell with zero capital gains. Most efficient transfer vehicle.
2
Roth IRA — Tax-Free Distributions
Heirs withdraw tax-free over 10 years. Can let it grow during the window. No RMDs on inherited Roth during 10-yr period.
3
529 Accounts — Qualified Withdrawals Tax-Free
Can change beneficiary to grandchildren. Roth conversion cap already used. No step-up in basis.
4
Pre-Tax (TSP, PRSP) — Ordinary Income on Distribution
Heirs pay income tax on every dollar withdrawn. Spread over 10 years to minimize bracket pressure. Convert to Roth before death where possible.
